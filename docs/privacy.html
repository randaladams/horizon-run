Horizon Run – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · App: Horizon Run for Android (com.adamselite.horizonrun)

The short version

  • Horizon Run has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • Your scores, stats and settings stay on your phone. We (the developer) do not receive or store them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads, as described below.

Who we are

Horizon Run is made by Randal Adams (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at randy@adamselite.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a small amount of information only on your phone:

  • your best score and a list of your last 10 runs (date and time, score, items collected, run length);
  • lifetime stats, your all-time top 10 and badges earned;
  • your settings (light/dark mode, sound, theme and mode) and whether you have finished the tutorial.

This information is not sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account under Google's own terms. You can delete it at any time with Scores → Reset scores in the game, or by uninstalling the app.

Motion sensor and vibration

The game reads your phone's tilt (accelerometer) to steer, and may vibrate briefly on events like a crash. Sensor readings are used instantly during play and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Horizon Run shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can choose to watch to keep running after a crash, and an ad between games every few games. To deliver, measure and limit ads (and to prevent fraud), Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped, and diagnostic data.

Google acts under its own privacy policy. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the Privacy button in the game's Scores screen.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

In-app purchase

The optional Full Version upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see or store your card or account details. The game only asks Google Play whether the upgrade is owned, so it can remove ads and unlock the full-version features (also after a reinstall). Once you own the Full Version, the game no longer loads ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers or analytics, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • The app requests no permissions beyond internet access (for ads) and vibration.

Children

Horizon Run is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the US such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled by the device controls above or by Google for ad data. You are always welcome to contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
